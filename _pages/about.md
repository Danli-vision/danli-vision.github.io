# Vision Intelligence Lab

<p class="lab-tagline">The Hong Kong Polytechnic University</p>

We develop foundation models, multimodal agents, and autonomous clinical AI systems to advance vision science and medicine, with a focus on ophthalmology and optometry.

I am a Research Assistant Professor at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. I earned my PhD at Zhongshan Ophthalmic Center under the supervision of [Prof. Mingguang He](https://scholar.google.com.au/citations?user=uVVC9F8AAAAJ&hl=en), and my BS and MS degrees from Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

# Research Directions

<div class="research-grid">
  <a class="research-card" href="#news">
    <span>Generative AI for Ophthalmology</span>
    <small>Generative models for retinal imaging, fluorescein angiography, indocyanine green angiography, and multimodal data.</small>
  </a>
  <a class="research-card" href="#news">
    <span>Medical AI Agents</span>
    <small>Interactive agents for clinical reasoning, diagnosis, visual question answering, information retrieval, and clinical decision support, including assistance for clinicians and patients using electronic health records.</small>
  </a>
  <a class="research-card" href="#rmhas">
    <span>Oculomics and Digital Biomarkers</span>
    <small>Retinal and choroidal biomarkers to predict systemic disease, assess population health, and inform preventive medicine.</small>
  </a>
  <a class="research-card" href="#service">
    <span>Autonomous Clinical Intelligence</span>
    <small>Human-robot collaboration, embodied AI, and clinical AI systems for the autonomous clinics of the future.</small>
  </a>
</div>

- **Generative AI:** [CF2FFA](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666914523001331), [CF2ICGA](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-024-01018-7), [FFA-GPT](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-024-01101-z), [EyeGPT](https://www.jmir.org/2024/1/e60063/), [EyeFound](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11338), [EyeCLIP](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-025-01772-2), [EyeWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.14039)
- **Medical AI agents:** [Evaluation](https://mednexus.org/doi/full/10.1016/j.imed.2025.03.002), [VQA](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjo-2024-326097), [ChatMyopia](https://www.cell.com/iscience/fulltext/S2589-0042(25)02029-2), [EyeAgent](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.09394)
- **Oculomics and digital biomarkers:** retinal vascular biomarkers from the <a href="#rmhas">RMHAS series</a> and choroidal biomarkers such as [ICGA fingerprints](https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.66.13.3)
- **Autonomous clinical intelligence:** [Human-robot collaboration](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13401152/), [Digital twin clinics](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.21416)

# Join Us

<div class="recruiting-container" aria-label="Recruiting information">
  <p>We are recruiting motivated PhD students, research assistants, interns, visiting students, and undergraduate researchers. We welcome applicants with backgrounds in computer science, biomedical engineering, AI, ophthalmology, optometry, medicine, and related fields.</p>

  <h2>PhD research with clinical relevance</h2>
  <p>Apply AI to research questions in vision science and medicine: develop models to generate and interpret multimodal ophthalmic images, build agents for clinical reasoning, discover biomarkers of systemic disease, or investigate human-robot collaboration in clinical care. Explore our <a href="#research">research directions and publications</a> to find opportunities that align with your interests.</p>
  <p>Our students have received the <a href="#funding">Kuok Group Fellowship and PPPFS</a>, and our alumni include a recipient of PolyU's Best Research Postgraduate Student award. Meet our <a href="#members">students and alumni</a>.</p>

  <h2>How to apply for HKPFS &middot; September 2027 entry</h2>
  <ol class="phd-steps">
    <li><strong>Discuss your research interests.</strong> Email <a href="mailto:danli.shi@polyu.edu.hk?subject=Prospective%20PhD%20%2F%20HKPFS%20-%20September%202027">Danli Shi</a> with your CV, academic transcript, a brief description of your research interests, and examples of your work. Use the subject line &ldquo;Prospective PhD / HKPFS + your name + university + research area&rdquo;. You must also complete the formal applications below.</li>
    <li><strong>Obtain an HKPFS reference number.</strong> Submit an initial application through the <a href="https://cerg1.ugc.edu.hk/hkpfs/InitApplication.jsp">RGC HKPFS system</a> by <strong>1 December 2026, 12:00 noon Hong Kong time (UTC+8)</strong>.</li>
    <li><strong>Submit your full PolyU application.</strong> Apply through <a href="https://rpgadmission.polyu.edu.hk/">RPgAdmission</a>, select &ldquo;2027/28 Semester 1 (Sept 2027 Entry)&rdquo;, and quote your HKPFS reference number by <strong>1 December 2026, 23:59 Hong Kong time (UTC+8)</strong>.</li>
  </ol>
  <p class="funding-note">Check the <a href="https://www.polyu.edu.hk/gs/hkpfs">official application guide and current deadlines</a> and <a href="https://www.polyu.edu.hk/study/pg/research-postgraduate/admission-requirements-rpg">PolyU admission requirements</a> before applying. Fellowship selection is competitive.</p>

  <div class="recruiting-requirements">
    <h3>Lab Selection Criteria</h3>
    <ol>
      <li>A strong interest in research, self-motivation, and leadership skills.</li>
      <li>A strong record in at least one of the following: undergraduate study at a highly ranked university, a high GPA, or multiple high-quality publications.</li>
    </ol>

    <h3>Suggested Application Materials</h3>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Email subject:</strong> Prospective PhD / RA / Intern + your name + undergraduate university + research area.</li>
      <li><strong>Attachments:</strong> A CV including your GPA or class rank, an academic transcript, and a research proposal outlining your background, research interests, and reasons for joining the lab.</li>
      <li><strong>Optional but helpful:</strong> Relevant awards (such as a National Scholarship or equivalent), representative publications, a thesis, code repositories, competition results, or project pages.</li>
      <li><strong>Funding:</strong> For PhD applicants, review the <a href="#funding">funding opportunities below</a> and the official scheme pages.</li>
    </ol>
  </div>
